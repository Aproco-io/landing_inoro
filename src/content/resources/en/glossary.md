      <h2>A</h2>
      <p><strong>ACD (Automatic Call Distributor)</strong> — the system that routes incoming calls to available agents based on rules (skills, priority, wait time).</p>
      <p><strong>Agent assist</strong> — AI that suggests responses or actions to agents during live calls. Distinct from post-call analytics like InOro, which analyzes calls after they end.</p>
      <p><strong>AHT (Average Handle Time)</strong> — talk + hold + after-call work time; average across a set of calls.</p>
      <p><strong>AML flag</strong> — regulatory indicator triggered by patterns suggesting anti-money-laundering risk in a conversation.</p>
      <p><strong>ASR / STT</strong> — Automatic Speech Recognition / Speech-to-Text: converts audio to text.</p>
      <p><strong>Auto-QA</strong> — automated quality assurance: AI scores every call against a scorecard instead of a human reviewing a sample.</p>
      <p><strong>Abandonment rate</strong> — share of calls where the caller hung up before reaching an agent.</p>
      <h2>C</h2>
      <p><strong>Call center analytics</strong> — measurement and reporting for a call center; can include phone-system, survey, and conversation-content metrics.</p>
      <p><strong>Call transcription</strong> — converting call audio into text, often with speaker separation.</p>
      <p><strong>Compliance monitoring</strong> — detection of regulatory or procedural violations in customer interactions.</p>
      <p><strong>Contact center</strong> — a broader term than call center; may include voice, email, chat and messaging.</p>
      <p><strong>Conversation analytics</strong> — analysis of customer conversations to extract topics, sentiment, quality signals.</p>
      <p><strong>Conversation intelligence</strong> — the AI-powered business layer built on top of speech analytics; scores, insights, actions.</p>
      <p><strong>CSAT</strong> — Customer Satisfaction Score, usually from a post-call survey ("How would you rate this call?").</p>
      <p><strong>CES</strong> — Customer Effort Score, measuring how easy it was to get a resolution.</p>
      <p><strong>Churn signal</strong> — language or behavior in a call suggesting the customer may leave.</p>
      <h2>D</h2>
      <p><strong>Diarization</strong> — separating audio by speaker (agent vs customer).</p>
      <h2>F</h2>
      <p><strong>FCR (First Contact Resolution)</strong> — share of issues resolved on the first customer contact.</p>
      <h2>I</h2>
      <p><strong>Interaction analytics</strong> — analytics across customer interactions; often used interchangeably with conversation analytics.</p>
      <h2>K</h2>
      <p><strong>Keyword spotting</strong> — older speech-analytics approach that flags predefined phrases; contrasted with contextual LLM analysis.</p>
      <h2>N</h2>
      <p><strong>NPS (Net Promoter Score)</strong> — a customer loyalty index based on the "would you recommend" question.</p>
      <h2>O</h2>
      <p><strong>Occupancy</strong> — the share of an agent's logged-in time spent on calls or wrap-up.</p>
      <p><strong>On-premise</strong> — deployment model where all software and data live on the customer's own infrastructure.</p>
      <h2>P</h2>
      <p><strong>PII anonymization</strong> — masking of personal information (national IDs, card numbers) in transcripts and analytics.</p>
      <h2>Q</h2>
      <p><strong>QA scorecard</strong> — a defined set of criteria and weights for evaluating a call's quality.</p>
      <h2>S</h2>
      <p><strong>Sentiment analysis</strong> — measurement of emotional tone in a conversation, usually per moment or per topic.</p>
      <p><strong>Service level</strong> — the share of calls answered within a target time (commonly 80/20 = 80% within 20 seconds).</p>
      <p><strong>Speech analytics</strong> — the audio-to-data layer: transcription plus structured analysis (topics, sentiment, phrases).</p>
      <h2>V</h2>
      <p><strong>VoC (Voice of the Customer)</strong> — customer feedback captured across channels, aggregated for insight.</p>
      <h2>W</h2>
      <p><strong>White-label analytics</strong> — the vendor's product delivered under the reseller's brand; standard for BPO conversation-analytics offerings.</p>
