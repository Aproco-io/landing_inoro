      <h2>From keyword spotting to LLMs</h2>
      <p>The shift from NLP (rule-based, phrase-driven) to NLU (contextual understanding) is the biggest change in the category in a decade. Older tools flag "cancel" as a churn indicator; modern LLM-based analysis reads "I wouldn't cancel" for what it is.</p>
      <h2>Sentiment analysis explained</h2>
      <p>Sentiment can be measured per call, per topic, or over the timeline of a single conversation. The timeline view is where the value is — it shows the moment a customer's mood shifted, tied to what the agent said.</p>
      <h2>Topic modeling & root causes</h2>
      <p>Automatic topic clustering answers "why are customers calling this week?" — with volume, priority and trend. Remove the top cause at the source and deflect the calls.</p>
      <h2>Predictive signals: real vs marketing</h2>
      <p>Real: churn signals from language cues (frustration + competitor mention + retention offer refused). Upsell intent from buying-related phrases. Marketing: end-to-end "predicts anything" claims — treat with skepticism, ask for the data.</p>
      <h2>Build vs buy</h2>
      <p>Building the whole stack (STT + diarization + LLM + scorecards + dashboards) is a multi-quarter project for a specialized team. Buying a platform gets you 90% of the value in weeks. Build if you're a data team with unusual requirements; buy otherwise.</p>
      <h2>Limits & failure modes</h2>
      <p>Honestly: audio quality dominates accuracy. LLM hallucinations happen on ambiguous transcripts. Calibration between AI and human reviewers matters — build a review workflow into the rollout.</p>
