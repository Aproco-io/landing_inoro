      <h2>Definition</h2>
      <p>Conversation intelligence (CI) is a class of software that applies AI — speech-to-text, NLP and large language models — to customer conversations. It converts audio into structured data, then extracts business meaning: what customers wanted, how it was handled, what happened next.</p>
      <h2>How it works</h2>
      <ol>
        <li><strong>Transcription:</strong> audio to text, with speaker separation.</li>
        <li><strong>Semantic analysis:</strong> topics, sentiment, quality scoring against a scorecard, compliance flags.</li>
        <li><strong>Aggregation:</strong> dashboards, trends, actionable alerts.</li>
      </ol>
      <h2>What it's NOT</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Not a voicebot — CI doesn't talk to customers.</li>
        <li>Not agent assist — CI runs post-call, not during the call (in most implementations).</li>
        <li>Not IVR — CI analyzes what happened, not how the call was routed.</li>
      </ul>
      <h2>Use cases</h2>
      <p>Quality assurance (auto-QA on 100% of calls), compliance monitoring, sales coaching, root-cause analysis of contact reasons, CX and NPS explanation.</p>
      <h2>Conversation intelligence vs speech analytics vs call analytics</h2>
      <p>Three related but distinct terms:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Speech analytics</strong> — the audio-to-data layer (transcription, phrase spotting, sentiment).</li>
        <li><strong>Conversation intelligence</strong> — the business layer on top (scoring, insights, action).</li>
        <li><strong>Call center analytics</strong> — the reporting/dashboard layer combining both.</li>
      </ul>
      <h2>How to evaluate a platform</h2>
      <p>Checklist:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Coverage: 100% or sampled?</li>
        <li>Languages: does your primary language work well? Test on your calls.</li>
        <li>Data residency: EU/on-premise options?</li>
        <li>Scorecard fit: can it map your existing QA sheet 1:1?</li>
        <li>Pricing model: per-seat vs per-minute — which matches your seasonality?</li>
        <li>Real-time or post-call? Post-call is standard; real-time is a different category.</li>
      </ul>
